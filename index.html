Video editor

What it does
Send a link or a file. It downloads the video, trims it to the part you want and writes the caption script for you to check. When you say burn, it adds captions phrase by phrase with the active word highlighted in your colour, then puts the finished file on Google Drive so you can watch it on your phone.
